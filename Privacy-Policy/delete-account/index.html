Request Account and Data Deletion

This page provides instructions on how to request deletion of your account and personal data for Teilen developed by Ahmed Mohamed Ali Mohamed.

How to Request Account Deletion

If you wish to delete your account and associated phone number/data, you can submit a request via email:

  • Email: ahmedmohamedalyyy@gmail.com
  • Subject: Account Deletion Request - Teilen
  • Required Information: The registered phone number linked to your account.

Requests will be processed and completely purged within 30 days of receipt.

What Data Will Be Deleted

  • Registered phone number and authentication credentials.
  • User profile details and preferences.
  • Any user-generated content or activity history linked to the account.

Data Retention

We do not retain any personal information after account deletion, except for basic transaction or security logs required by law or necessary fraud prevention, which are deleted after 90 days.
