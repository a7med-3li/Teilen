Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Ahmed Ali Mohamed ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, stores, shares and deletes information when you use the Teilen mobile application (package name com.teilen.app), the Teilen web app, and any related services (together, the "Service").

Teilen is a personal sharing inbox. You send content to yourself from your phone (links, text, photos, PDFs and other files) and open it on any device that you have paired to the same account. Shared items are temporary and delete themselves automatically after the lifetime you choose. By downloading, installing or using the Service, you agree to this Privacy Policy.

2. Summary

  • Teilen has no advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • We do not sell your personal data, and we do not share it for advertising.
  • We do not collect your location, contacts, calendar, call logs, SMS, health data, or biometric data, and we do not access your camera or your photo library.

  • The clipboard feature is off by default, opt-in, and never sends anything automatically. Nothing is transmitted until you tap "Send".

4. How We Collect Information

  • Directly from you: when you enter your phone number, name a device, approve a pairing, or send content.
  • Automatically: server logs and device "last seen" times are produced as a normal part of serving requests.
  • Through the Android share sheet: when you choose Teilen in the system "Share" menu, the app receives only the item(s) you selected for that share.

6. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal data and we do not share it for advertising. We share information only in the limited cases below:

  • Between your own paired devices. The whole point of Teilen is to move your content from one of your devices to another. Content you send is delivered to devices you have paired to your account.
  • Service providers (hosting). The backend and stored files run on Amazon Web Services (AWS) that processes data on our behalf solely to operate the Service. These providers are bound by contract to protect the data and use it only for that purpose.
  • Google Play / operating system. When you install the app through Google Play, Google processes data related to the store and app distribution under its own privacy policy. The app itself does not send your data to Google.
  • Legal requirements. We may disclose information if required to do so by law, or in response to a valid legal request, if we believe disclosure is necessary to comply with a legal obligation or to protect rights and safety.
  • Business transfer. If the Service or relevant assets are transferred to another owner, information may be transferred as part of that transaction; this Policy would continue to apply until updated.

Apart from the cases above, other users cannot see your content, and another account cannot access it even if it knows an item's identifier — such requests are rejected as "not found".

7. Data Retention and Deletion

  • Shared content is temporary by design. Every item has an expiry time that you choose when sending. The default is 30 minutes, the minimum is 10 seconds, and the maximum is 24 hours. When it expires, the item and any uploaded file are automatically and permanently deleted from our server.
  • You can delete sooner. You can delete any item immediately from the app or web feed. Deletion removes both the database record and the stored file.
  • Account and device data (phone number, display name, device names, hashed tokens) is kept while your account is active.
  • Pairing codes live for about 5 minutes and are stored only as hashes; expired pairing requests are swept automatically.
  • Server logs are kept for a short period for security and troubleshooting, then deleted or anonymized.
  • Account deletion. You can request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by emailing ahmedmohamedalyyy@gmail.com. We will delete the account, its devices, and any remaining content. Because your phone number is your account identifier, deleting the account releases the number. Note: the last paired device cannot unpair itself from within the app (to prevent locking the account), so account deletion is done by request.

8. Security

We take reasonable and commercially acceptable measures to protect your information:

  • Encryption in transit. The app and web app communicate with the server over HTTPS/TLS.
  • Hashed tokens. Device access tokens are stored only as one-way SHA-256 hashes, so a database leak does not expose usable tokens.
  • Hashed pairing codes. Pairing codes are single-use, short-lived, and stored only as hashes.
  • Signed file links. File downloads use tamper-proof, expiring signed links that cannot be edited to reach a different item or a later expiry.
  • Strict access control. Every content request is authenticated and filtered to your account only; other accounts receive "not found".
  • Least data. We collect only what the Service needs, and content is deleted automatically.

No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. If you believe your account has been compromised, contact us immediately and revoke the affected device from the account screen.
