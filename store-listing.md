## Full description (2796/4000 characters)

Teilen is a personal share inbox for your own devices. Send a link, a note, a photo or a PDF to yourself from your phone, and it shows up on your other devices in real time — then deletes itself when its time is up. No emailing yourself, no chat app cluttered with your own messages, nothing to clean up later.

HOW IT WORKS
• Capture — use your phone's normal Share button from any app (browser, WhatsApp, Telegram, Gmail, your file manager) and pick Teilen. Nothing new to learn.
• Retrieve — open Teilen on another device you've paired once, or just open the web app in your browser. Your item is already there.
• Expire — every item has a lifetime you choose. When it ends, the item and its file are removed from the server automatically.

WAYS TO SEND
• Share sheet — any content type: text, links, images, PDFs and other files (up to 30 MB each). Selecting several files creates one card per file, each with its own timer.
• Copy to Teilen — shows up in the text-selection toolbar of almost any app. Send the text you selected and stay exactly where you were.
• Send what I copy — optional. Turn it on and, when you copy something, a notification offers a one-tap Send. It never sends anything on its own.
• In the app — paste text or pick a file, choose how long it should live, and send.

PAIRING THAT JUST WORKS
Create your account with your phone number and your first device is paired on the spot. To add another device, scan one QR code — or type a short code — and approve it from a device that is already paired. Pair once and it keeps working across sessions. You can see every paired device and revoke any of them at any time.

BUILT TO LEAVE NO TRACE
• Everything expires. The default is 30 minutes; choose anywhere from 10 seconds to 24 hours.
• Delete any item instantly from the app or the web feed.
• Device access tokens are stored only as one-way hashes.
• Shared files are served through tamper-proof, expiring links.
• Strict per-account isolation: other accounts cannot reach your content, even with an item's ID.

PRIVATE BY DEFAULT
Teilen has no ads, no third-party analytics and no tracking. We do not sell your data. The app does not ask for your location, contacts, camera or media library — it only receives the exact file you choose to share.

A NOTE ON ACCOUNTS
Your phone number is your account. Whoever claims a number first owns it, so please use your own number.

Teilen is for sending things to yourself, not for messaging other people — a quiet, private bridge between your own devices, and the fastest way to move something from your phone to your laptop without cluttering either one.
